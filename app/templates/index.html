About This Tool

This prototype demonstrates a DRM benchmarking system based on the World Bank's framework described in "Driving Policy Reform for Disaster Resilience: Lessons from World Bank DPF Cat DDOs (2025)". The tool provides a methodology for assessing DRM governance through policy typology, benchmarking indicators, and maturity levels to support assessment, reform guidance, and iterative improvement.
